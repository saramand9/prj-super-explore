# Community code of conduct

Explore welcomes constructive participation regardless of experience,
background or identity. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests,
reviews and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code respectfully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Give actionable feedback and assume that other participants may be learning.
- Respect different experiences, accessibility needs and communication styles.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, private files or
  credentials without consent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing and persistent disruption are not acceptable.

## Moderation and reporting

The repository maintainer, [@saramand9](https://github.com/saramand9), may edit or
remove harmful content, limit participation, close discussions or block accounts
to protect the community. Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and
its impact, with context considered where appropriate.

For ordinary moderation questions that can be discussed safely, contact the
maintainer in a project issue without including private details. For abuse that
requires a private report, use
[GitHub's private abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
That process is handled by GitHub, not a project-operated confidential inbox.
The maintainer must establish a project-private contact before promising one.

Report security vulnerabilities according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not as
public moderation issues. Retaliation against good-faith reports is unacceptable.
